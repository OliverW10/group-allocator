Privacy Policy

"The tool" refers to the Group Allocator web application available at https://group-allocator.com

Upon sign in to the tool using the google OAuth integration, it collects the 1. Email address and 2. Full name, associated with the google account.
Upon submission of the form shown when signed in as a student the tool stores data entered into the form such as: Selected preferences for project, personal statement, uploaded files.
When signed in as a teacher, data uploaded or entered to configure a "class" is stored, this includes: projects details, student emails, clients details.

This data required for the function or legitimate interest of the tool in assigning students to project groups based on preferences.

The data is not shared with any third parties, and is stored on a server located in Australia.

Data is retained until it's associated "class" is deleted via the tool.

Compaints and Deletion requests can be emailed to oliver.warrick@student.uts.edu.au
